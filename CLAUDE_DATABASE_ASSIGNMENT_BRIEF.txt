Business rules already implied by the schema
- Each user has a role: STUDENT, PARENT, COUNSELOR, or ADMIN.
- A student profile belongs to exactly one user and a user can have at most one student profile.
- A parent can be linked to multiple students and a student can be linked to multiple parents through parent_student_links.
- Assessments are created by users and contain many questions.
- Each question belongs to one assessment and can have many options.
- Student assessments connect a student to an assessment attempt and store score and status.
- Student answers belong to a student assessment and a question.
- Aptitude profiles are derived from assessment results for a student.
- Careers are matched to aptitude profiles and can be mapped to skill clusters.
- Colleges offer degrees, and degrees may optionally link to a career.
- Decision plans recommend a degree and optionally a career for a student.
- Counselors are assigned to students and can produce reports.
- Labour market data stores yearly career market snapshots.
- Admin logs, feedback, and sessions support auditing, quality control, and security.

Normalization notes for the 3NF section
The schema is mostly organized in a third-normal-form style, but Claude should document the following carefully instead of claiming everything is perfectly normalized without review.
- Good 1NF/2NF/3NF candidates: users, student_profiles, assessments, questions, question_options, student_assessments, student_answers, careers, colleges, degrees, counselor_assignments, admin_logs, system_feedback, user_sessions.
- Tables that are clearly reporting or derived and may be justified as controlled denormalization: counselor_reports, career_matches, aptitude_profiles, labour_market_data, decision_plans.
- Potential normalization caveat: student_constraints stores preferred_locations and preferred_career_paths as VARCHAR lists. If these are comma-separated values, they are not fully atomic and should be called out as a normalization risk. A stricter design would split them into child tables.
- Another caveat: fields such as career.required_aptitude_cluster and aptitude_profiles.skill_cluster are descriptive lookup-style values and should be documented as domain attributes, not duplicated facts.
- The report should show why each non-key attribute depends on the key, and should mention that the schema avoids obvious repeating groups in most tables.
